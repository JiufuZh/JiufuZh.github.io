      Transparency and message processing in health supplement promotion on Instagram

      August 2024–May 2025

      Advised by Prof. Archana Krishnan and PhD candidate Alex R. Stankus.

      • Designed and refined a codebook for 417 Instagram posts promoting branded health supplements. Variables covered sponsorship disclosure, influencer type, product category, and message-processing routes from the Elaboration Likelihood Model (ELM).
      • Multi-stage content analysis, with intercoder reliability of 0.95–1.00, revealed missing sponsorship labels and heavy reliance on peripheral-route persuasion cues among influencers in the sample.
      • Used statistical analysis and exploratory work in Python to examine associations among influencer characteristics, disclosure transparency, and message-processing strategies.

      An extended abstract I co-authored was accepted for presentation at the 112th NCA Annual Convention, November 2026.

      Research listing in adviser's CV (PDF)

      The February 2026 CV lists the related work “Unlabeled influence” as a COMET submission. The NCA link opens the conference website.

      Wastewater-derived struvite for sustainable agriculture: characterization and controls on nutrient release

      UAlbany ESE Research Lab · Research Aide
      February–May 2025

      Advised by Prof. Rixiang Huang and PhD candidate Shyrill F. Mariano.

      • Tracked how three types of struvite dissolved in organic acids and deionized water over 24 hours using inductively coupled plasma (ICP) measurements, with pH recorded at seven time points.
      • Soil column experiments simulated environmental conditions, with ICP measurements used to assess dissolution.
      • Cleaned and processed the ICP and pH datasets, then visualized the results to investigate factors affecting dissolution.
