Engineering notes +

What I worked on

A responsive analysis interface. I ran Stockfish in a Web Worker and added request cancellation and result versioning, so changing positions would not leave an old result on screen.

Feedback grounded in the game. I added structured-output validation, checks against engine evidence, and fallbacks for API failures. Explanations adapt to the player's rating and language; saved reviews reopen without another LLM call.

Fog of War chess AI

I prepared training data and trained policy-and-value models for chess with a partially visible board. My work also covered self-play, resumable training, and browser integration.

Python · PyTorch · FastAPI · JavaScript

Internship project · illustrated training and inference workflow

From recorded games to a browser move: replay 1,554 games and 88,949 plies, construct player-visible observations, split examples by complete game, train a policy-and-value model, save resumable self-play checkpoints, and connect local inference to a browser script.

Engineering notes +

What I worked on

Keeping hidden information out of the input. I constructed player-visible observations and masked opponent histories. Splitting by complete game keeps positions from the same game together during training and evaluation.

Connecting training to use. I trained policy-and-value models in PyTorch and worked on self-play and checkpoint resumption. The FastAPI service and JavaScript browser script carry observations into inference and return the selected move to the board.
